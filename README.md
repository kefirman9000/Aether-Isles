# Aether Isles: Swarm of the Warden

A bright, magical swarm-runner for PC and mobile browsers. You start as a lone Arcane Warden on a crumbling floating isle. Steer through glowing multiplication gates (×2, ×3, ×5…), grow a tidal wave of light constructs, and blast through the Shadow Blight.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser, or play on GitHub Pages at https://kefirman9000.github.io/aether-isles/

## How to play
- **Steer:** drag left/right (touch or mouse), or use A/D or the arrow keys.
- **Gates:** pass through the left or right half of each gate. Blue gates help, red gates hurt.
- **Fight:** your swarm fires on its own. Blight hordes that reach you trade one-for-one with your constructs.
- **Overcharge:** once your swarm is 20+ and the meter is full, tap the button (or press Space) for 5 seconds of triple fire.
- **Boss:** every isle ends with a Blight Colossus. Dodge its pink slam circles.

## Features
- **Weapon Evolution Gates:** switch the whole swarm to Crystal Spears, Arcane Waves or Star Orbs, or level up the weapon you already have.
- **Champion Echoes:** absorb glowing figures for 10 seconds of Aegis shield, chain lightning, a speed burst or starfall.
- **Isle Fragments:** collect ✦ pieces during runs and place them in your Sanctuary. Every 4 placed adds +1 to your starting swarm, and each Rune Shrine adds +3% damage.
- **Blight Mutations:** every 3 runs the Blight changes. It might explode on death, split when slain, ward itself against Light Bolts, or rush in faster.
- **Dream Runs:** harder, mirrored versions of isles you've cleared. Each first clear unlocks a new construct look.
- **Summary export:** Settings → Export summary (also on the end-of-run screen). Copy it, add your notes, and send it in to request changes.
- **Updater:** Settings → Check for updates. It only contacts GitHub when you accept.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage). You can move it between devices with Settings → Export/Import save.

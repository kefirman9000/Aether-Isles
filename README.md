# Gridlock City

A top-down open-city crime game in the spirit of the original 2D GTA games, for PC and mobile browsers. Steal cars, answer ringing payphones for jobs, cause chaos and try to lose the cops.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser, or play on GitHub Pages at https://kefirman9000.github.io/Aether-Isles/

> This repo used to hold *Aether Isles: Swarm of the Warden*. It's been replaced for now; the old game is still in the git history (commit `85a66b8`).

## Controls
| PC | Phone | Action |
|---|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | Left-thumb joystick | Walk · drive (W gas, S brake/reverse) |
| Mouse + left click | FIRE (auto-aims) | Aim and shoot |
| F / E / Enter | CAR | Get in, get out, or jack a car |
| Space | DRIFT | Handbrake |
| Q / mouse wheel | GUN | Switch weapon |
| P / Esc | ‖ (top right) | Pause |
| M | | Mute |

## Features
- **Procedural city:** an 8×8 grid of blocks with leaning, GTA2-style 3D buildings, parks, parking lots, a hospital and a police station.
- **Traffic and pedestrians:** cars follow lanes and turn at intersections; people walk the sidewalks, cross roads and scatter when things get loud.
- **Driving physics:** grip, handbrake drifts, skid marks, crash damage, smoke, fire and explosions. Six car types, from the little Pipsqueak to the Viper and the Hauler truck.
- **Wanted level (1–5 stars):** cops chase you through the streets, get out to arrest you on foot, and start shooting at 3+ stars. Stay out of their sight to drop stars.
- **Spray shops ($500):** drive onto a red-and-yellow pad to clear your wanted level, repair and repaint your car.
- **Payphone jobs:** Special Delivery, Hit the Golden Viper, Briefcase Run and Hot Potato (lose 3 stars), each on a timer.
- **Weapons and pickups:** pistol, SMG and rocket launcher crates, health and cash scattered around town.
- **Wasted / Busted:** respawn at the hospital or the police station and pay the bill.
- **Updater:** Menu or pause → Check for updates. It only contacts GitHub when you press it.

Your cash is saved in your browser (localStorage).
